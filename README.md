# CareerCraft AI

**Google XYZ Resume Scorer • Job Tailor • Letters Generator • Deep AI Humanizer**

CareerCraft AI is an all-in-one web application designed to optimize your job applications using Google's proven XYZ formula and bypass robotic AI cadence.

---

## 🌟 Key Features

1. **Google XYZ Resume Scoring & Bullet Breakdown**:
   - Scores accomplishments based on Google's formula: `Accomplished [X] as measured by [Y], by doing [Z]`.
   - Analyzes action verb strength ([X]), quantifiable data density ([Y]), and execution methodology ([Z]).
   - Bullet-by-bullet interactive grading with instant Google XYZ rewritten versions and "Copy" shortcuts.
   - Comprehensive diagnosis: Overall Score (0-100), Letter Grade (A+, A, B, C, D), Strengths, and Weaknesses.
   - ATS match score against target job descriptions with Matched and Missing Keyword analysis.

2. **Resume Rewriting for Job Match**:
   - Takes your resume and target job description to produce a tailored, high-converting resume.
   - Boosts ATS keyword alignment from low match (~35–50%) to 90%+.
   - Converts weak bullets into high-impact Google XYZ bullets.
   - Side-by-side Before & After comparison.
   - Direct export to Microsoft Word (`.docx`), plain text (`.txt`), or clipboard.

3. **Cover Letter & Formal Application Letter Generator**:
   - Generates **both** a compelling narrative **Cover Letter** and a formal corporate **Job Application Letter** (Letter of Intent).
   - Features validated Google XYZ metrics directly in the body text.
   - Customizable tones: Confident & Results-Driven, Tech & Modern, Executive & Strategic, Formal & Traditional.
   - One-click export to Word (`.docx`) and `.txt`.

4. **Deep AI Humanizer (Text & Document Support)**:
   - Paste text or upload documents (`.pdf`, `.docx`, `.txt`).
   - Removes AI clichés and tells (*"delve into"*, *"testament to"*, *"orchestrated"*, *"seamlessly"*, *"tapestry"*).
   - Injects authentic sentence length variation (Burstiness).
   - Multiple modes: Professional Polish, Conversational & Direct, Academic Rigor, Stealth Bypass.
   - Before & After AI Likeness meter (e.g. 96% AI reduced to 4% Human-like).
   - Export humanized documents directly to Word (`.docx`).

5. **Universal Document Support**:
   - Supports both **File Upload** (`.pdf`, `.docx`, `.doc`, `.txt`, `.md`) and **Direct Text Paste** across all tools.
